PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
Multi-Tenant Attendance & Workforce Management Platform
Version: 1.0

1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
A multi-tenant SaaS platform for organizations to manage employees, attendance, GPS/geofencing, leave, branches, locations, policies and reports.

2. OBJECTIVES
- Automate employee attendance.
- Validate attendance using GPS and geofencing.
- Support multiple organizations and administrators.
- Support branches and multiple work locations.
- Provide leave management and reports.
- Support offline attendance and synchronization.
- Provide a scalable SaaS foundation.

3. USER ROLES
Super Admin: manages the SaaS platform, organizations, plans and subscriptions.
Organization Owner: manages one customer organization.
HR Admin: manages employees, leave and attendance.
Attendance Admin: manages attendance and locations.
Branch Manager: manages assigned branch employees and attendance.
Employee: check-in/out, attendance, leave and profile.

4. CORE FEATURES
Authentication: login, logout, refresh token, password reset, JWT.
Organization: create, update, activate/deactivate, settings.
Employees: create, update, deactivate, branch and role assignment.
Attendance: check-in/out, GPS validation, geofence validation, late, overtime and manual attendance.
Locations: coordinates, radius, branch assignment.
Leave: leave types, applications, approval/rejection, balance and history.
Reports: daily, monthly, employee, branch, leave, late and overtime.
Offline: local attendance queue and server synchronization.

5. MULTI-TENANCY
Every customer organization is an isolated tenant. Organization-owned records must contain organization_id and API authorization must restrict access to the authenticated organization.

6. BUSINESS MODEL
The product can be sold as SaaS using plans based on employees, administrators, locations and features.

7. MVP
Multi-tenancy, authentication, RBAC, multiple admins, employees, branches, GPS attendance, geofencing, leave, basic reports, offline attendance and synchronization.

8. FUTURE
Subscriptions, payment gateway, advanced analytics, payroll integration, notifications, shift management, biometric verification and public APIs.
